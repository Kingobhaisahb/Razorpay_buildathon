# ShopControl

### Autonomous AI Growth Platform for Online Merchants

ShopControl is an autonomous AI growth platform designed to help online merchants identify and act on revenue opportunities across their commerce funnel.

Instead of relying on a chatbot or static analytics dashboard, ShopControl continuously analyzes merchant data, detects growth opportunities, uses AI agents to reason about those opportunities, and recommends controlled actions within merchant-defined financial guardrails.

---

##  Problem

Online merchants generate large amounts of commerce data across:

- Products
- Customers
- Orders
- Checkout sessions
- Payment methods
- Devices
- Offers
- Experiments

However, identifying actionable revenue opportunities from this data often requires manual analysis.

A merchant may have:

- High mobile checkout abandonment
- Poor performance from a specific payment method
- Products receiving many views but few conversions
- Offers that are not producing enough revenue
- Checkout experiments that could improve conversion

ShopControl aims to turn these signals into actionable, measurable growth decisions.
